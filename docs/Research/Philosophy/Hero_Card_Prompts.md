# Hero Card — Image Generation Prompts

Companion to `Hero.md` (the concept doc — read that first for the "why"). This document holds the actual image-gen prompts used to explore the collectible character card concept.

Status: exploratory. Not a locked direction — see `Hero.md`'s Open section.

Each variant below is a standalone prompt, no shared boilerplate between them. All three keep the same simple cartoon territory and the same basic card idea (name at top, generic waving placeholder character in the middle, level indicator at bottom) but each describes a genuinely different, simpler cartoon style. Character is always a generic placeholder, no specific facial likeness, swapped for a custom design later. Plain text, no markdown formatting inside the prompts themselves.

---

## Variant 1 — Flat Vector Cartoon

Clean sticker-style flat illustration, like a modern app icon or mobile game avatar. Uniform bold outlines, flat solid colors, no gradients, no shading beyond maybe one flat highlight shape.

A simple portrait card. At the top, a small banner reading VLAD PETRE in a bold rounded font. In the center, a generic cartoon character with a friendly, simple design, waving at the viewer, drawn in a clean flat vector style with even line weight and solid flat colors, no gradients, no realistic shading. At the bottom, a small plate reading LVL and a number. Warm, friendly color palette, a few flat solid colors, nothing neon or pastel washed out. Simple background, mostly a single flat color. Keep the whole image simple and clean so it reads well at a small size.

---

## Variant 2 — Bold Poster Cartoon

Chunkier, more graphic cartoon style, like a flat animated show poster. Thicker outlines, bigger simplified shapes, higher contrast flat colors, a little exaggerated in proportion, still completely flat, no gradients, no painterly shading.

A simple portrait card. At the top, a bold banner reading VLAD PETRE in a chunky graphic font. In the center, a generic cartoon character, simplified and slightly exaggerated proportions, waving at the viewer, thick confident outlines, big flat color shapes, no gradients, no realistic shading. At the bottom, a plate reading LVL and a number. Punchy, high contrast flat colors. Plain, simple flat color background so the character stays the focus. Keep it graphic and bold, not busy.

---

## Variant 3 — Hand-drawn Doodle Cartoon

Loose, playful, hand-drawn cartoon style, like a notebook doodle brought to life. Slightly wobbly, imperfect ink lines instead of perfectly clean vector lines, still bold and confident, flat colors, no gradients, no realistic shading.

A simple portrait card. At the top, a hand-lettered style banner reading VLAD PETRE. In the center, a generic cartoon character, simple and friendly, waving at the viewer, drawn with loose expressive ink linework and flat colors, no gradients, no realistic shading. At the bottom, a small plate reading LVL and a number. Warm, simple color palette. Plain background, kept minimal. Keep the whole thing feeling handmade and playful, not messy.

---

Run all three and compare. Note which one actually feels right before treating any of this as decided, then update `Hero.md`'s Open section.
