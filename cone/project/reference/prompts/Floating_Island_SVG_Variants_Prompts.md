# Floating Island SVG — 5 Variant Prompts

## Variant 1: Moss-Rock Island

```markdown
Design a floating island as pure SVG for a game portfolio's hero background — the pedestal a character stands on. Match this visual language: thick black ink outlines (#1C1610) on every shape, flat saturated color fills with no soft gradients or realistic shading, and chunky hard-offset drop shadows like a bold arcade/comic sticker. Animate only via CSS `transform`/`opacity` — never toggle `display`, animate layout properties, or use SMIL motion paths.

Give it a wide, stepped rock mass — dark slate (#1E293B to #475569) at the base, capped with a bright mossy green crown (#4ADE80 to #15803D) that overhangs the edges slightly, like turf draped over stone. The underside should be jagged and uneven, tapering into broken outcrops and dangling roots that trail off into nothing rather than a clean flat bottom — this thing is torn out of the ground and floating in the void. Let a couple of thin waterfalls (#ECFDF5) spill off the lip and vanish below, and scatter a faint ring of glowing rune-like motes (#A7F3D0) where the moss meets the rock, like the island is held aloft by old magic rather than physics.

Make it feel alive rather than posed: a slow, continuous turn around its own vertical axis, unhurried, like a diorama on a museum turntable rather than a spinning coin — always readable, never edge-on. Layer in smaller independent motion underneath that — the waterfalls trickling, the dust motes drifting upward and fading, maybe the moss swaying faintly — so the island still feels like it's breathing even at the rotation's calmest point. You have creative freedom on exact shapes, proportions, and how you build the rotation illusion — the brief is the mood and material, not the geometry.
```
