### 1. The Secrets of "Soulful Artisanal Craft" (Micro-Detail Directives)

To make every component feel alive and full of story, implement these exact micro-details:

#### A. Environmental Storytelling & Hand-Crafted Touches
- **Pencil & Compass Measurement Marks**: Faint pencil tick marks, guild draftsman grid lines, and angle compass arcs on wooden surfaces and paper margins.
- **Coffee / Tea Ring Stains**: Light, semi-translucent circular tea stains (`rgba(160, 95, 40, 0.08)`) sitting subtly under parchment cards.
- **Tattered Paper & Deckle Edges**: Parchment is never a flat rectangle—add deckled edges, dog-eared folded corners, torn scroll ends, and aged fibrous paper textures.
- **Wax Drips & Guild Stamps**: Wax seals feature organic wax drip droplets on the paper surface and embossed emblem crests inside. Add ink stamp marks ("GUILD APPROVED", "RANK S", "CRAFTED IN TAVERN").
- **Physical Hardware Details**: Brass screws and rivets feature **randomized slot rotations** (-45°, +30°, +85°) so they don't look machine-cloned. Add stitched thread loops on leather straps and patina highlights on metal corners.
- **Magical Glints & Whimsical Sparkles**: Glass potion flasks glint with white light reflections, and enchanted items emit subtle CSS/motion golden sparkle bursts on hover.

#### B. Chunky, Tactile 3D Depth & Bouncy Motion
- **Chunky 3D Extrusions**: Buttons and cards have thick, physical 4px–8px bottom extrusions (`border-b-4`, `box-shadow` stacking) that physically press downward (`translate-y-[4px]`) on click with snappy `motion/react` spring physics.
- **Layered Z-Elevation**: Floating parchment notes cast soft ambient occlusion drop shadows onto the wood canvas underneath. Hanging shop signs sway gently with spring damping.
- **Crisp Dark Ink Outlines**: Use rich, warm dark ink outlines (`#1E150C` or `#2C1A0E`) for a crisp, hand-illustrated storybook aesthetic.
