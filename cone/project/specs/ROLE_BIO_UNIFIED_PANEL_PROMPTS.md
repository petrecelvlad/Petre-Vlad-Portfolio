# Prompt 1 — Stretched Nameplate Panel

Copy everything between the lines below into a separate tool/agent.

---

Design and build a single SVG UI panel for a friendly, colorful mobile game's hero/profile screen.
It sits in a narrow-to-medium vertical column, below a large name title (not part of what you're
building — leave clear space above for it) and above a call-to-action button (also not part of
what you're building — leave clear space below for it).

This panel must hold **three pieces of text**, unified into one cohesive object rather than
separate stacked cards:

1. A small label word: **"ROLE"**
2. A short title/value line right after it, larger: **"GAME PRODUCER"**
3. A paragraph of body text below that: **"Game Producer & Senior Designer with 19+ years creating
   engaging games, leading cross-functional teams, and crafting memorable player experiences across
   mobile and casual platforms."**

**The core design problem:** an earlier version had the role (label + title) as one small floating
tag/badge, and the bio paragraph as a separate card underneath it, with a visible gap between the
two — it read as fragmented, like two unrelated stickers placed near each other. Your job is to
design ONE unified shape where all three text pieces live inside the same continuous silhouette —
not two things that happen to be adjacent.

## Concept: stretched nameplate-into-panel

Build this as **one single traced outline, start to finish, no seams**:

- It starts at the top as a **small tab**, narrow, holding the "ROLE" label. Big, soft, rounded top
  corners.
- That tab's sides **flare outward smoothly** as they descend — a gentle inward curve (like the
  rounded notch on a puzzle piece, a soft molded-together join, not a sharp corner and not a bump)
  — into a **wider nameplate band**. This band holds "GAME PRODUCER" in large bold rounded text.
- The nameplate band's bottom edge **continues directly, with no gap or seam**, into a **larger
  panel body** beneath it — same outline, same fill family, no visual break — which holds the bio
  paragraph.
- The whole shape (tab + nameplate band + panel body) is traced as one continuous, chunky, rounded
  outline from top to bottom — every corner soft and pillowy, nothing sharp or angular anywhere.

## Visual style: bright, chunky, flat "cute casual mobile game" UI

This must look like it belongs in a friendly, colorful mobile puzzle/idle/collection game — think
a reward popup, an inventory tag, or a profile card from that kind of game. It should NOT look
futuristic/sci-fi, NOT look like a gritty fantasy-RPG document, and NOT look like a serious
trading-card-game holo card. Follow these rules closely:

- **One thick, uniform, dark outline** traces every shape, with consistently soft/rounded corners —
  never a sharp point or a thin corner anywhere, even at small joins.
- **Flat colors only, no gradients, no glow, no blur.** Each region of the shape is a single solid
  color.
- **At most two tones per region for "3D":** a main flat color, plus one single darker flat band
  along the bottom edge (or one edge) of that region, like a simple flat "rim" — not a gradient,
  not multiple shading bands, not a painterly highlight. Think of a rounded candy button that's one
  color on top and one slightly darker color in a clean strip along its bottom rim.
- **A crisp, hard-edged flat drop shadow**, offset straight down a short distance behind the whole
  shape, in a single dark flat color — no blur, no soft glow, just a clean flat silhouette shadow
  (a "sticker sitting slightly above the background" look).
- **Palette: bright, saturated, candy-like colors** — sky blues, mint or lime green, warm orange,
  buttery yellow, soft pink or purple, cream/tan for any paper-like areas. Avoid dark, moody, or
  metallic tones; avoid muted "aged leather/parchment" browns as a dominant color; avoid anything
  that reads as neon, glowing, or high-tech.
- **No repeating micro-patterns or fine texture anywhere** — no hatching, no engraved linework, no
  fabric/paper grain, no small repeated decorative motifs. Every fill is a clean, flat block of
  color.
- **Typography is bold, chunky, and rounded/bubbly** — friendly game-UI lettering, not a thin
  elegant font and not a gothic/fantasy font. Text is usually white or cream, optionally with a
  simple flat outline or a tiny flat drop-shadow of its own.
- **Keep decoration minimal and bold, not detailed.** If you add any accent shape or icon, make it
  one single, simple, instantly-readable shape — never a cluster of small ornamental details.
- **Three to five flat colors total** for the whole panel, including the outline. A bold main color
  plus one or two supporting/accent tones reads as "part of a friendly game system"; more than that
  starts to feel busy.

## Text placement

- "ROLE" centered in the small top tab.
- "GAME PRODUCER" centered in the nameplate band, sized clearly larger/bolder than "ROLE."
- The bio paragraph left-aligned or centered (your call) inside the panel body, with comfortable
  margins on all sides — it's 2-4 lines of text at a normal reading size, so size the panel body
  generously enough that it doesn't feel cramped.

Text doesn't need to be rendered as part of the SVG unless your tool requires it — reserving the
space and describing where each piece goes is enough.

## Deliverable

A single SVG (or SVG-producing output) of this unified panel, proportioned for a vertical column
roughly 320-480px wide, tall enough to comfortably fit all three text pieces with room to breathe.

  line or stacked. This banner should look **stuck onto the notepad**: overlapping the card's top
  edge by a small amount, so it visibly sits on top of the note rather than floating separately
  above it with a gap.
- The banner and the notepad card must share the same thick outline color and the same flat
  drop-shadow direction/offset, so even though they may be two overlapping shapes, they clearly
  read as one assembled sticker-on-notepad object, not two coincidentally-stacked pieces.

This must look like it belongs in a friendly, colorful mobile puzzle/idle/collection game — think
a reward popup, an inventory tag, or a profile card from that kind of game. It should NOT look like
a serious classified document, NOT look gritty or aged, and NOT look futuristic/sci-fi. Follow these

  along the bottom edge (or one edge) of that region, like a simple flat "rim" — not a gradient,

  assembly, in a single dark flat color — no blur, no soft glow, just a clean flat silhouette shadow

  buttery yellow, soft pink or purple, cream/tan for the notepad paper. Avoid dark, moody, or
  metallic tones; avoid anything that reads as aged/vintage paper, neon, or high-tech.
- **No repeating micro-patterns or fine texture anywhere** — no hatching, no engraved linework, no
  paper-grain or fabric texture, no small repeated decorative motifs. Every fill is a clean, flat
  block of color, including the notepad's "paper" area.
- **Typography is bold, chunky, and rounded/bubbly** — friendly game-UI lettering, not a
  typewriter/monospace document font and not a gothic/formal font. Text is usually white or cream,

  heart, or dot near the banner) is plenty — never a cluster of small ornamental details or a

- **Three to five flat colors total** for the whole panel, including the outline. A bold banner

  system"; more than that starts to feel busy.

## Text placement

- "ROLE" small, "GAME PRODUCER" large, both centered within the sticker-tag banner at the top.
- The bio paragraph inside the notepad card below the banner, in friendly rounded text, with
  comfortable margins on all sides — it's 2-4 lines of text at a normal reading size, so size the
