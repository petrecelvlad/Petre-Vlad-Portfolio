# Tab 1 (Overview) copy drafts

Parent work: [T-046](../roadmap/board/T-046-rewrite-project-copy.md) (copy) and
[T-044](../roadmap/board/T-044-project-tabs-standardization.md) (tab structure).

## Format rules

- **Facts strip:** Studio · Team · Platform · Stage · Duration. Omit a slot only if it truly doesn't apply.
- **Project line:** one line, what the game is and its scale.
- **Role line:** one line, what was owned. Reframe toward producer-relevant skills (ownership, cross-team work, data-driven decisions, shipping, mentoring) — never retitle a design role as a producer role; titles get checked against LinkedIn.
- **Impact bullets:** 2–3, outcome first, numbers where they exist, no design jargon. Two is fine when a third would be padding.
- **Voice:** resume voice — no "I", past tense, plain words.
- **No repeats:** a bullet shouldn't restate the achievement card (`achievements[0]`) word for word. Flagged per project below.
- Budget: fits ~8 ruled lines under the clipboard header.
